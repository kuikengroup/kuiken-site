## Guardrails

- Never invent results, revenue, lead counts, testimonials, or client claims.
- Do not publish empty routes or controls that look interactive but are not.
- Do not add CMS, authentication, analytics, or external services without an implementation requirement.
- New sections must earn their place by improving trust, comprehension, or conversion.
